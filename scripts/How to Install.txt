CLIPBOARD HISTORY — HOW TO INSTALL
==================================

Keeps everything you copy (text, images, files) saved on your Mac,
so you can paste it again later, even days afterwards.
Everything stays on your Mac. Nothing is sent to the internet.

Needs macOS 14 (Sonoma) or newer. Works on Apple Silicon and Intel Macs.


1. INSTALL
   Drag "Clipboard History" onto the "Applications" folder in this window.


2. OPEN IT THE FIRST TIME
   Open your Applications folder and double-click "Clipboard History".

   macOS will probably say it "can't be opened" or "Apple could not verify" it.
   That's normal for apps that don't come from the App Store. To allow it:

     a. Click "Done" (or "OK") on that message.
     b. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security.
     c. Scroll down. Next to "Clipboard History was blocked…", click "Open Anyway".
     d. Enter your Mac password and click "Open Anyway" again.

   You only need to do this once.

   Still won't open? Open the Terminal app, paste this line, press Return,
   then double-click the app again:

     xattr -cr "/Applications/Clipboard History.app"

   Or skip all of this: paste this line into Terminal and press Return.
   It downloads, installs and opens the latest version for you:

     curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/10shaikhnuman-hue/clipboard-history/main/install.sh | bash


3. USE IT
   • Copy anything as usual (⌘C, screenshots, "Copy Image" in a browser…).
   • Press ⌘⇧V (Command + Shift + V) anywhere to see your history.
   • Pick an item and press Return (or double-click it) to paste it.
   • Opening the app again from Applications or Spotlight also shows the history.
   • There's also a clipboard icon in the menu bar at the top of the screen.
     On MacBooks with a notch it can be hidden when the menu bar is full;
     ⌘⇧V always works.


4. OPTIONAL
   • Paste for you: the first time you pick an item, macOS asks for
     "Accessibility" permission. Allow it and the app pastes for you.
     Without it, the item is copied and you just press ⌘V yourself.
   • Start automatically: click the menu bar icon → "Open at Login".
